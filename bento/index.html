PROJECT HAIL MARY

2026 Film

A thrilling sci-fi, with humorous human connection with an alien.

PLOT

In the near future, middle school teacher and former molecular biologist Ryland Grace wakes from an induced coma aboard the interstellar spacecraft Hail Mary, suffering from amnesia. He learns that he is the sole survivor of a three-person crew travelling to the Tau Ceti system, 11.9 light-years from Earth.

Through flashbacks, Grace recalls scientists discovering a "Petrova line" of infrared light between the Sun and Venus. An alien microbe called "astrophage" was proliferating on the Sun, dimming it at a rate that would cause catastrophic global cooling within thirty years. Eva Stratt, head of the Petrova Taskforce, recruited Grace because of his expertise in speculative astrobiology.
